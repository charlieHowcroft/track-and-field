about

Specialising in Track and Cross Country, Field Recruit offers a premium service of college recruiting and mentoring built around a passion for helping athletes develop both in and out of their sport.

Over Sam’s six-year tenure in the NCAA, including his own recruitment and two transfers to different schools, he became extremely familiar with the recruiting process, the college landscape and what it takes to find the right fit.

Field Recruit is built around the belief that having the right people investing in an athlete’s innate drive can make a career changing difference. Sam has experienced this himself, through the teachings of some of the nation’s best coaches while also being surrounded by excellent mentors, friends and teammates who have gone on to become world medallists, national champions and Olympians.

We want to give athletes access to the information, experience and mentorship that Sam wishes he had throughout his own journey. This includes exclusive insight from high-level athletes and Sam’s own experiences, helping athletes understand what worked, what didn’t and how they can make the most of their own opportunities.

Our advice and mentoring can range from nutrition, training, mental performance, altitude, lifestyle and navigating the NCAA, to the broader mindset and habits required to develop as an athlete and as a person.

the NCAA

National Collegiate Athletic Association

what is the NCAA?

The NCAA is the main governing body for university sport in the United States. The organisation provides a structured pathway for athletes to combine university education with competitive sport.

who is part of it?

The NCAA is comprised of more than 1,000 university institutions across three divisions. These institutions compete in organised sporting competitions across a wide range of sports including Track and Field, Cross Country, swimming, basketball, soccer and tennis.

what does it do?

The NCAA facilitates university teams travelling student-athletes to competitions and hosting NCAA-sanctioned meets. It also establishes and governs eligibility requirements, organises national championships and regulates how universities recruit and support student-athletes.

how do the divisions differ?

The three divisions offer different levels of competition, university environments, academic opportunities and scholarship structures. Understanding these differences is an important part of finding the right university and opportunity for each athlete.
