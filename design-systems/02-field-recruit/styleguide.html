08 disclosure

what is the NCAA?

The NCAA is the main governing body for university sport in the United States. The organisation provides a structured pathway for athletes to combine university education with competitive sport.

how do the three divisions differ?

The three divisions offer different levels of competition, university environments, academic opportunities and scholarship structures. Understanding these differences is an important part of finding the right university and opportunity for each athlete.
